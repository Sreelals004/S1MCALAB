DIWALI

Diwali is the hindu festival of lights, with variations celebrated in other Indian religions such as jainism and sikhism.It symbolieses the spiritual victory of Dharma over Adharma,light over darkness, good or evil, and knowledge over ignorance.Diwali is celebrated during the hindu lunisolar months of ashvin and kartika-between around mid-october and mis-November.The celebrations generally last five or 6 days.
