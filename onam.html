ONAM

Onam is the official cultural and harvest festival of kerala,celebrated as vibrant ten-day event during the malayalam month of chingam.The festival marks the joyous annual homecomming of the legendray,benevolent Asura KIgn Mahabali,whose reign is remembered as a golgen era of complete equality,peace and properity,The festivities begin on the Star Atham and peak on the final, most asuspicious day known as Thiruvonam.





During these ten days.homes are adorned with Pookkalam,which are intricate,colorful floral carpets laid at entrances to welcome the spirit of King Mahabali.A central highlight of the celebration is the Onasadya, a grand,traditional vegetarian feast consisting of up to 26 district dishes served on a fresh banana leaf.The festival is also cleribrated with spectacular community events,including the thrilling vallam kali held on Kerala's rivers and lively traditional folk arts such as the PUlikali and Kaikottikali dance performaces
